Building in Frederick County

Rooted in Frederick County. Committed close to home.

Steve Howard grew up in Frederick County, Maryland, and has always loved the area. Square One is now intentionally focusing closer to home.

Frederick County covers a lot of ground. There are the historic streets of Downtown Frederick and Baker Park, the ridges of Braddock Heights, the open land of the Middletown Valley, and the country properties around Jefferson, Urbana, Ijamsville, New Market and Lake Linganore.

More of the people building here are moving up from Bethesda and DC. They want land and privacy, and they want the same kind of architect-designed modern home they would have built closer to the city. That is the work we do.

Exceptional architecture. Built with experience.

Homes we’ve built for leading architects have received national AIA honors, International Architecture Awards, and recognition in publications including Dwell, Interior Design, and Architectural Record. Those awards belong to the architects. The work behind them is board-formed concrete that is also the finished wall, exposed steel, large glass, and details with no trim to hide behind. It is the same standard we bring to a house in the valley or a renovation downtown. See the portfolio and the Blue Ridge House story.

ADUs, pool houses and guest suites

We also build smaller buildings: backyard cottages, pool houses, guest and in-law suites, and detached studios, on Frederick lots and on acreage. As an ADU builder in Frederick, MD, we treat a small building with the same care as the main house, with the same architect, the same detailing and the same site work done by our own crews. Examples include a recent pool house ADU in Alexandria, VA, designed by MODE4 Architecture, and the guest house at Blue Ridge House for Olson Kundig, where a rolling wall with a Murphy bed on each side lets one room work as an office, a studio or a two-bedroom suite.

Rural lots, handled from day one

Building on acreage is different from building in town. Before the house comes the site: septic, well, grading, driveways and steep slopes, plus the permits and inspections that come with them. Square One owns its own excavation and heavy equipment, so our crews handle lot clearing, rough grading and foundation excavation in-house. We don’t wait on someone else’s schedule to get started, and we coordinate the perc testing, septic, well and civil work with the architect and engineer from the beginning.
