Three Notes

MODE4 Architecture · Built by Square One Development Group
The Project

A mid-century house, renovated and expanded.

Three Notes is in the Fort Hunt neighborhood of Alexandria, Virginia. MODE4 Architecture renovated and expanded a mid-century, developer-built house on a half-acre wooded site, to 6,921 square feet. Its vertical towers draw on musical notation.

Square One Development Group was the builder.

An outstanding example of transformative residential design.

Sarah Armstrong, jurist, in Home & Design
